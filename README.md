### A little about me

I'm a Computer Science undergrad who enjoys building things and figuring out how they work under the hood.

I reverse-engineered my university portal to build a mobile companion app, experimented with computer vision, and built backend services using Go, Redis, and PostgreSQL.

These days, I'm working with Rust, parsers, and data structures. Outside work, I'm usually building something, solving DSA problems, or trying to understand a technology I probably didn't need to explore at 2 AM.

I gravitate towards backend development and systems programming. I like understanding the problem first, figuring out the moving parts, and making the whole thing work.

### LinkForge

A URL shortener that grew into a playground for backend engineering.

Built with Go and Fiber, with PostgreSQL for persistence and Redis for caching. Includes rate limiting, click analytics, asynchronous processing, and monitoring.

`Go` `Fiber` `Redis` `PostgreSQL` `Docker`

[Explore the repository](https://github.com/pragyanbhatt1213/LinkForge--url-shortener-)

<td width="50%" valign="top">

### Chemical Equipment Visualizer

Upload equipment data, analyse unusual readings, and get a clearer picture of equipment health.

The project combines a Django REST backend with a React interface, PostgreSQL storage, IQR-based outlier detection, risk classification, and reporting.

`Django REST` `React` `PostgreSQL` `Docker` `Pandas`

[Explore the repository](https://github.com/pragyanbhatt1213/chemical-equipment-visualizer)

### VTOP Plus

My university portal was an interesting engineering problem.

With no public API to rely on, I worked with session-based authentication, cookies, CSRF tokens, and HTML parsing to retrieve academic information for a Flutter app.

`Flutter` `Dart` `HTTP` `HTML Parsing`

[Explore the repository](https://github.com/pragyanbhatt1213/VTOP-Plus)

A computer vision project for detecting crowds in video streams.

Built around YOLOv8 and OpenCV, with GPU acceleration as part of the performance work.

`Python` `OpenCV` `YOLOv8` `CUDA/GPU`

[Explore the repository](https://github.com/pragyanbhatt1213/Crowd-Scanner)
